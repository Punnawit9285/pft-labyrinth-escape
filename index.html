Case 1 (IPF) first, then asthma, COPD, lecture and lab examples. Works on phones and computers.

How to play

  1. 3 hearts. Each wrong answer costs one. Lose all three and the stage restarts.
  2. Every junction asks two things. First: which value do we look at here? (for example FEV₁/FVC). Then: which corridor? Pick the door whose rule matches the patient, for example ≥ 75% or < 75%.
  3. Items, any time. Case shows the patient’s report (open it to read the numbers). Map is the class flowchart with the answers covered. Ref has the formulas. Peeking under a cover resets your streak.
  4. Three right in a row earns a booster: Immune (the next mistake is free) or Healer (+1 heart).
  5. Reach an exit to escape. The exit names the spirometry pattern, then the case is explained.
